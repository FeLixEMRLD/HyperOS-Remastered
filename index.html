Fastboot Installation Guide

Step 01

Prerequisites

Ensure your device's bootloader is completely unlocked. Back up all vital internal storage data, as installation will factory reset your system. Download the Fastboot ROM zip file specifically engineered for your hardware variant and extract it entirely onto your computer.

Step 02

Enter Fastboot Mode

Power down your device completely. Hold down the Power Button and the Volume Down Button simultaneously until the screen switches over to the official Fastboot construction screen. Once visible, securely link your device to the computer using an optimal USB interface cable.

Step 03

Execute Installation Script

Open the extracted Fastboot archive directory folder on your desktop. Locate the flashing initialization script optimized for your environment: run windows_fastboot_first_install_with_data_format.bat on Windows architectures, or launch macos_linux_fastboot_first_install_with_data_format.sh via terminal parameters on UNIX systems.

Step 04

Finalize Initialization

A confirmation text console will deploy. Confirm terminal script deployment parameters and wait patiently as the terminal patches system imagery partitions. Once internal operations conclude successfully, the terminal wrapper close command triggers automatically, forcing your device to initialize into HyperOS Remastered.

Critical: Never extract or deploy flashing script architecture tools directly on USB hubs or volatile storage networks. For seamless system deployment and to prevent accidental execution errors, maintain extraction files locally in safe root environments.
