Remastered is the pure, base HyperOS REMASTERED, without any compromises. Remastered SWIFT however, disables Advanced textures and replaces Live blur with Static, Lite blur for efficiency and less lag. It also replaces the Remastered launcher with our internal, Custom Lightweight version for stability.

  • Texture Refactoring Removed Advanced textures to increase performance and reduce lag.
  • Sleek Blur Integration Replaced the live control center blur with a sleek, clean lite blur that doesn't eat up much performance.
  • System Optimization Simplified the animations of the control center, and replaced the System launcher with a more lightweight, internal Launcher to reduce stutters.
